# CONSTRAINTS
- **Culinary Authenticity**: Must be strictly vegetarian and recognisably South Indian. Use real, named traditional dishes (e.g., Idli with Sambar, Ragi Malt, Keerai Masiyal, Chapati with Kurma) rather than generic descriptions or Westernized health foods (no quinoa, avocado, or kale).
- **Flexible Calorie & Portion Logic**: Provide two distinct daily caloric and meal structures:
  1. **Workout Days**: Higher energy/protein target optimized for performance and recovery.
  2. **Non-Workout Days**: Adjusted, lower caloric target designed to offset a sedentary desk-bound schedule without extreme restriction.
- **Local Sourcing & Simplicity**: Ingredients must be easily available in local South Indian markets. Recipes must be realistic, affordable, and quick to prep for busy professionals.
- **Balanced Nutrition**: Ensure adequate protein, fiber, and micronutrients while maintaining a safe daily caloric deficit (approx. 400–600 kcal below TDEE).

# PLAN
Create a complete 7-day meal plan (Monday through Sunday) covering Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, and Dinner, complete with specific portion sizes and explicit differentiation between Workout Day and Non-Workout Day targets.

# FORMAT
Present the final output using the following structure:
1. **Calorie & Macro Targets Summary**: TDEE, Deficit Targets, and Macros for Workout vs. Non-Workout days.

- Assuming rigid daily training schedules without built-in flexibility for zero-workout days.
